# Generative AI Use Disclosure

## Tool used

**ChatGPT (OpenAI)**

## How AI was used

ChatGPT assisted with the design and drafting of Google Colab/Python code for:

- Loading and preprocessing the arXiv summarization dataset.
- Fixed-size and sentence-aware chunking.
- Sentence-transformer embeddings and ChromaDB indexing.
- Baseline similarity retrieval and cross-encoder reranking.
- Conversational-memory query rewriting.
- Relevance-label collection, metric computation, and chart generation.
- Debugging, output interpretation, and preparation of the experiment report and documentation.

ChatGPT also reviewed selected relevance judgments and proposed corrections. The final reported retrieval metrics were recalculated using the revised judgments.

## Human involvement

The student executed the notebook in Google Colab, reviewed intermediate outputs, entered initial relevance judgments, and provided experiment results for review. AI-assisted code and interpretations were checked through notebook execution and output inspection; independent validation of every judgment and full end-to-end reproducibility is not claimed.

## Transparency and limitations

- The evaluation is based on 100 documents, two initial questions, and one follow-up question.
- Relevance judgments were human-entered initially and subsequently AI-assisted during auditing.
- Reported results are retrieval metrics, not measurements of generated-answer correctness.
- The use of ChatGPT does not replace responsibility for verifying the code, results, and final submission.

## Declaration

Generative AI was used for coding assistance, debugging, relevance-label review, evaluation support, and documentation. The experiment was run in Google Colab.
